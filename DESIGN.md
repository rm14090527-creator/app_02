## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated farm-to-table editorial ethos: grounded, deliberate, and luxuriously organic. It bridges the sensory abundance of harvest markets with the exacting restraint of high-end editorial gastronomy. 

### Brand Personality & Demeanor
- **Cultivated & Artisanal:** Prioritizes deliberate craft, provenance, and botanical authenticity over processed perfection.
- **Editorial & Warm:** Speaks with the literary quietude of an independent culinary publication, avoiding loud commercial urgency.
- **Lush & Nourishing:** Uses rich natural pigments and unhurried whitespace to inspire vitality and conscious indulgence.

### Target Audience
Discerning food enthusiasts, conscious culinary curators, and wellness pursuers who seek uncompromised organic sourcing, high-integrity ingredients, and a visually serene, tactile mobile culinary experience.

### Visual Movement
**Organic Editorial Modernism:** A hybrid of classical botanical journals and clean contemporary digital interaction. Tactile warm-cream surfaces serve as a resting canvas for deep woodland green structures, balanced by warm sun-baked terracotta accents, deliberate hairline dividers, and high-craft serif headlines anchored by structural geometric sans-serif details.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical soil, chlorophyll pigments, and sun-baked earth, deployed across warm organic parchment foundations.

### Strategic Color Roles
- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Deep Woodland Forest):** Anchors foundational structures, high-emphasis text, primary interactive triggers, and structural navigation. Imparts depth, longevity, and organic authority.
- **Secondary (`#40916C` - Vibrant Sage):** Communicates vitality, freshness, vegetative life, and positive progress. Deployed for secondary controls, selected chip fills, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#E76F51` - Sun-baked Terracotta):** Represents earth, warmth, and harvested fruit. Used strategically for promotional markers, culinary highlights, alerts, and vital conversions.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` - Crisp Charcoal):** Retains ink-like precision for high-density reading and dense data sets without the harsh synthetic severity of pure black.

### Surface Canvas Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBFA`):** An unbleached, creamy linen tone establishing warmth across primary mobile viewports.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#F4F3EE`):** Warm oat parchment, creating gentle architectural stratification behind cards and contextual sections.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#D8F3DC` - Mint Light Tint):** Fresh spring wash reserved exclusively for nutritional verification callouts, organic provenance seals, and success notifications.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial pairing between classical gastronomy and clean modern utility.

### Type Pairings & Editorial Rhythm
- **Headlines (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, heritage, and human scale. Editorial titles use normal or medium weights to highlight natural letterforms and organic curve transitions.
- **Body & Metadata (Outfit):** Delivers geometric precision, pristine legibility at small sizes, and open aperture counters that keep ingredient listings and nutritional metrics effortless to scan.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- Headlines larger than `headline-md` must switch to their `-mobile` equivalents on narrow viewports to preserve delicate line balances and eliminate hyphenation breaks.
- Nutritional badges and micro-labels utilize `label-sm` with uppercase transformations and expansive letter-spacing (+0.06em) to evoke botanical taxonomy plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first spatial model engineered around tactile thumb navigation and spacious reading rhythm.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile (Base to 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` inter-column gutters. Cards sit wide with prominent product photography.
- **Tablet (600px to 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with `2rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product cards transition into multi-column collections.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at an architectural maximum container width of `1280px`, centering content with `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
Interface components maintain multiples of `0.25rem` (4px). Standard micro-gaps between headers and subtitles strictly utilize `space-xs` (4px) or `space-sm` (8px), while content clusters deploy `space-md` (16px) to maintain editorial connection before dividing with `space-xl` (36px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh, unpigmented drop shadows in favor of ambient, chlorophyllic warmth and physical tonal tiers.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Bed (Lowest):** `#FBFBFA` canvas background.
2. **Elevated Surfaces:** Surface elements are rendered on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale oat (`#F4F3EE`), establishing structural distinction without hard boundaries.
3. **Overlays & Modals:** Floating sheets and drawers utilize softened pure white backgrounds accented with muted forest perimeter boundaries.

### Ambient Shadow Architecture
- **Resting Product & Recipe Cards:** Subtle, diffused grounding shadow tinted with forest undertones:
  `box-shadow: 0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.03);`
- **Active & Dragged Surfaces:** Expanded presence with soft elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04);`
- **Sticky Bars & Persistent Navigation:** Supported by a soft, warm blur accompanied by a hairline border:
  `border-top: 1px solid rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08); background: rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.92); backdrop-filter: blur(12px);`

## Shapes

The shape system relies on balanced organic curvatures that feel refined, natural, and welcoming rather than technical or industrial.

### Geometry Archetypes
- **Standard Cards & Containers:** Configured to `1rem` (16px, `rounded-lg`) on mobile viewports, stepping up to `1.5rem` (24px, `rounded-xl`) on larger hero containers and recipe modules.
- **Controls & Input Fields:** Uniform `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded`) radius to provide crisp, reliable spatial containers for operational interactions.
- **Badges, Pills & Indicators:** Dynamic pill shapes (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for nutritional qualification badges, category filters, and harvest seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled deep woodland forest (`#1B4332`) with white text. Height: 52px on mobile for generous thumb targets. Typography: `label-lg`. Padding: `0 1.5rem`. Corner radius: `0.5rem`.
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined container with a 1.5px solid border in `#1B4332`, transparent background, and `#1B4332` text.
- **Tertiary / Promo Button:** Sun-baked terracotta (`#E76F51`) background with crisp white text, reserved for direct conversion paths, basket additions, and limited harvest drops.

### Chips & Category Filters
- **Filter Pills:** Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 9999px`) with an oat background (`#F4F3EE`) and neutral charcoal text (`#1F2937`). Height: 36px. Padding: `0 1rem`.
- **Selected State:** Shifts to solid deep forest (`#1B4332`) with white text, or vibrant sage (`#40916C`) when indicating an active nutritional filter.

### Badges (Nutritional Claims)
- **Claim Badges ('100% Orgánico', 'Sin Azúcar Añadida', 'Gluten Free'):** 
  Constructed using a mint light tint canvas (`#D8F3DC`) bordered with an ultra-thin hairline (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.15)`).
  Typography: `label-sm` rendered in `#1B4332`. Height: 24px. Padding: `0 0.625rem`. Pill-rounded.

### Cards
- **Product & Recipe Cards:** 
  White (`#FFFFFF`) or soft oat (`#F4F3EE`) background. Border radius: `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px).
  Contains a 1:1 or 4:5 organic food photo aspect ratio, clipped to the top radius. Internal content padding: `1.25rem`. 
  Ambient green-tinted shadow keeps the card grounded on warm cream foundations.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Text Inputs:** 
  Height: 48px. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.15)`. Border radius: `0.5rem`. 
  Focus state: 1.5px outline in primary green (`#1B4332`) with an ambient green outer glow (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.1)`). Placeholder text in soft charcoal (`#6B7280`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Selection Controls:** 
  Size: 20px x 20px. 
  Unchecked: 1.5px border in `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.3)` on white canvas. 
  Checked: Solid `#1B4332` fill featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark or inner dot. Radii: `4px` for checkboxes, full circle for radios.

### Micro-Components (Seasonal Origin Tag)
- Small hairline botanical tag highlighting local grower provenance (e.g., *Huerto Los Olivos — Cosecha de Hoy*), set in italicized `Newsreader` (`body-sm`) alongside a subtle sage leaf indicator.
